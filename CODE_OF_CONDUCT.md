# Community expectations / コミュニティの約束

Japanese and English contributions are welcome. Be precise about ideas and respectful
of people. Explain the evidence for a disagreement; do not attack a contributor's
identity, background or ability. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, publishing
private information and undisclosed promotional spam are not acceptable.

Do not pressure people to reveal team secrets or personal circumstances. Ask permission
before naming reviewers. Distinguish coaching experience, a source's claim and inference.
AI-assisted work receives the same evidence, rights and testing review as other work.

Maintainers may ask for edits, pause discussions, or restrict contributions to protect
the project. Raise a non-sensitive concern with the repository owner. For concerns
requiring privacy, request a private channel without posting the details publicly.
No response-time or dedicated private reporting service is promised.
